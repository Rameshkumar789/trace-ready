What Friday4:30 does

Friday4:30 is an incident response platform for food brands and manufacturers. The article says it helps companies manage and log issues from first alert to resolution. The founders describe the pain as the “Friday 4:30 moment” — when a product issue, production problem, recall, or supply-chain crisis appears late in the day and the company has to respond ad hoc.  

The platform is built around preparedness and structured incident management. It replaces crisis manuals, spreadsheet/email chains, and institutional knowledge that might live only in an experienced employee’s head. The article says it helps bring together the many moving parts of incident response and makes professional crisis management accessible to mid-market food brands.  

They raised £335,000 from Haatch, and the investor quote is important: Haatch specifically says food and drink companies are still managing product recalls and supply-chain crises on spreadsheets and email chains.
