  Raw transcripts and pasted analysis from the research conversation.

  Food traceability market map, startup landscape, and workflow maps.

  Startup thesis, YC-style framing, roadmap, competitor differences, and blindspot solutions.

- `partners/enseso4food/`  
  Jim White / ENSESO4Food analysis, call plans, talk track, and partner positioning.
