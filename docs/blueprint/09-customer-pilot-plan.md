# Customer Pilot Plan

## Pilot Offer

Name:

> TraceReady Audit

Offer:

> Send a redacted workbook/export plus supporting records. We return a FSMA 204 readiness gap report showing covered product scope, missing KDEs, lot-code lineage risks, supplier data-quality gaps, source-system readiness, sortable export readiness, and next remediation steps.

The first pilot should be **event/export-first**:

- TraceReady workbook,
- ERP/WMS export,
- EDI 856 / ASN sample,
- ENSESO4Food/TrackKey/ReposiTrak/Trustwell/Wholechain export,
- DProduce Man/Famous/QuickBooks/internal spreadsheet export.

Supporting documents are still useful:

- invoices,
- BOLs,
- packing slips,
- labels/photos,
- supplier PDFs,
- imported/non-English records.

## First Pilot Price

Options:

1. Free design partner audit  
   Use only if they commit to feedback call and allow anonymized learnings.

2. $250-$500 starter audit  
   Best first paid validation.

3. $1,500-$2,500 deeper audit  
   Use after sample report is credible.

Recommendation:

Start with:

> $500 pilot audit, waived for first 3 design partners if they provide real records and feedback.

## Who To Target First

Priority 1:

- medium produce distributors,
- packers/repackers,
- food hubs,
- fresh-cut processors,
- commissary kitchens.

Priority 2:

- food safety consultants,
- ERP/WMS implementers,
- traceability platform partners,
- produce associations.

Avoid:

- tiny buyers with no urgency,
- large enterprise procurement first,
- companies asking for full system replacement.

## Outreach Message

Subject:

> Quick FSMA 204 readiness check for produce records

Message:

Hi {{Name}},

I am building TraceReady Audit, a small FSMA 204 readiness review for produce and food operators.

The pilot is simple: you share a redacted workbook/export plus any supporting invoices, labels, BOLs, ASNs, or supplier documents. We return a short report showing:

- which products may be covered,
- which supplier/KDE fields are missing,
- whether lot-code lineage appears preserved,
- whether transformation records are linked,
- whether your source systems can carry the required fields,
- whether your records can support a sortable FDA-style export,
- what should be fixed first.

I am not asking you to switch systems. The goal is to show whether your current records are traceability-ready before audits, recalls, or platform onboarding.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call?

Best,  
Ramesh

## Discovery Questions

Ask:

1. What system do you use for receiving, inventory, and traceability today?
2. Do suppliers send lot codes, harvest dates, source locations, or ASNs?
3. Are case labels scanned, typed, photographed, or ignored?
4. If a customer asked for traceability data tomorrow, how would you produce it?
5. Do you know which products you handle are on the Food Traceability List?
6. Do you transform, repack, relabel, or commingle product?
7. Who owns FSMA 204 readiness internally?
8. Would a red/yellow/green gap report be useful?
9. What would make it credible?
10. Would you share redacted records for a pilot?
11. Can your ERP/WMS/EDI/traceability system export event-level records today?
12. Do any supplier records arrive in another language?
13. Are supplier lot codes exact, inferred, range-based, or sometimes missing?
14. Do mixed pallets, broken cases, or commingled lots happen in normal operations?

## Pilot Intake Checklist

Ask for:

- TraceReady workbook or existing export,
- 5-20 receiving records,
- 5-20 shipping records,
- invoices,
- BOLs,
- labels/photos,
- ASNs if available,
- EDI 856 sample if available,
- ERP/WMS/DProduce Man/Famous/QuickBooks export if available,
- item master,
- supplier list,
- transformation records if applicable,
- current traceability plan if they have one.
- imported/non-English records if they appear in the workflow.

## Pilot Success Criteria

Good signal:

- customer shares records,
- report finds real gaps,
- customer says output is accurate,
- customer asks "what do we do next?",
- customer wants supplier follow-up,
- customer asks whether TraceReady can monitor the same gaps continuously,
- customer asks whether TraceReady can map their ERP/WMS/EDI export,
- customer pays or agrees to paid next step.

Weak signal:

- customer only says "interesting",
- no records shared,
- no urgency,
- no owner,
- no willingness to pay.

## Follow-Up After Report

Ask:

1. Which findings were most useful?
2. Which findings were wrong or unclear?
3. Would you have paid for this?
4. What price would feel reasonable?
5. Do you want us to help fix the gaps?
6. Which suppliers cause the most repeated issues?
7. Who else should see this report?
