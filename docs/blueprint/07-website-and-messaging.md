# Website And Messaging

## Website Goal

The first website should not look like a generic SaaS landing page.

It should do one job:

> Get qualified food operators, consultants, and platform partners to request a TraceReady Audit pilot.

## Recommended Domain Options

Check availability before purchase:

- traceready.com
- gettraceready.com
- tracereadyaudit.com
- traceready.ai

Best practical MVP domain:

> gettraceready.com

## Homepage Hero

Headline:

> Find your FSMA 204 traceability gaps before an audit, recall, or platform onboarding.

Subheadline:

> TraceReady Audit reviews your products, suppliers, shipment records, lot-code workflows, and transformation data to show where your current process is not yet traceability-ready.

Primary CTA:

> Request a sample audit

Secondary CTA:

> See what we check

## Positioning Section

Title:

> Inside-the-walls traceability is not always FSMA 204 readiness.

Body:

Your ERP or warehouse process may know what came in and what went out. But FSMA 204 readiness also depends on supplier KDEs, traceability lot code preservation, transformation linkage, downstream sharing, and audit-ready records.

TraceReady helps you see the gaps before they become implementation, audit, or recall problems.

## What We Check

Cards:

1. Covered product scope  
   Which products appear to fall under the Food Traceability List?

2. Supplier KDE flow  
   Which suppliers need to provide traceability information, and what is missing?

3. Lot-code lineage  
   Are incoming traceability lot codes preserved unless transformation occurs?

4. Transformation linkage  
   Are input lots connected to output lots during repacking, processing, or transformation?

5. Data-sharing readiness  
   Can you produce customer/FDA-ready traceability records when needed?

6. Remediation plan  
   What should your team, suppliers, or systems fix first?

## How It Works

Step 1:

> Send sample records.

Copy:

Upload or share redacted item lists, supplier lists, receiving/shipping samples, invoices, BOLs, ASNs, labels, or transformation records.

Step 2:

> We run TraceReady Audit.

Copy:

We review covered product scope, KDE completeness, supplier gaps, lot-code lineage, transformation linkage, and data-sharing readiness.

Step 3:

> Receive a clear gap report.

Copy:

You get a red/yellow/green readiness report, supplier scorecard, and remediation checklist.

Step 4:

> Decide what to fix next.

Copy:

Fix internally, work with consultants, onboard a traceability platform, or use TraceReady Remediation to manage recurring gaps.

## Do Not Say On Website Yet

Avoid:

- "fully automated compliance"
- "certified FSMA compliant"
- "we replace traceability platforms"
- "AI solves traceability"
- "instant audit"
- "guaranteed FDA readiness"

## Short Website Footer Disclaimer

TraceReady Audit is a preliminary readiness review and is not a legal opinion, certification, or substitute for professional regulatory advice.
