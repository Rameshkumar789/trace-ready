# Jobs Demand Map For AI Traceability Exception Desk

## Executive Summary

The jobs market strongly validates your idea.

Companies are already hiring people to do pieces of your workflow:

- FSMA 204 traceability readiness
- supplier traceability communication
- KDE/CTE data exchange
- vendor issue routing
- traceback and recall request handling
- traceability KPI reporting
- mock recall governance
- inventory/lot/barcode data quality
- QA, EDI, receiving, and supplier coordination

The clearest job-market signal found is **Performance Food Group's Product Traceability Data Specialist** role. That role is extremely close to your product idea.

The job description includes:

- monitoring an FTR204 mailbox
- communicating traceability requirements to vendors
- maintaining vendor and customer traceability contacts
- acting as liaison between PFG, Trustwell, suppliers, and customers
- responding to traceback or recall requests
- troubleshooting vendor/customer traceability issues through Jira
- maintaining the Food Traceability Plan
- growing into leadership over FTR204 compliance and third-party KDE exchange systems

That is almost exactly the human workflow your software would automate or augment.

## Core Finding

Your software is not replacing one clean job title.

It is replacing and coordinating work spread across several roles:

| Role Type | What They Do Today | How Your Software Helps |
|---|---|---|
| Product Traceability Data Specialist | Handles FTR204 data, vendor communications, traceability issues, KDE exchange, recall/traceback requests | Becomes the operating system and agent desk for this role |
| FSQA / Traceability Manager | Owns traceability program, mock recalls, KPIs, supplier compliance, regulatory readiness | Gives them dashboards, exceptions, proof, and reports |
| EDI / ASN Analyst | Handles structured supplier/customer data exchange and transaction errors | Repairs ASNs and flags missing KDE fields |
| Supplier Compliance Specialist | Chases suppliers for documents, specs, corrective actions, and traceability readiness | Automates supplier follow-up and scorecards |
| Inventory / Receiving / Warehouse Ops | Captures lot, quantity, product, location, receiving data | Flags bad receiving records and item/lot mismatches |
| Recall / Regulatory Coordinator | Responds to tracebacks, recalls, withdrawals, and FDA/customer requests | Generates proof packets and sortable spreadsheet exports |
| Consultant / Readiness Assessor | Audits current state and gives FSMA 204 readiness recommendations | Productizes the audit and recurring exception workflow |

### Simple ROI Model

Assume a distributor has:

- 1 traceability data specialist at $80K salary
- fully loaded cost around $105K-$120K
- 2-4 QA/EDI/receiving people spending partial time on traceability exceptions
- 100-500 supplier traceability issues per month

If your software reduces manual work by 30-50%, the buyer may justify:

- $2K-$5K/month for a smaller distributor
- $5K-$15K/month for a multi-site distributor
- $20K+/month for enterprise/multi-facility operations if you own recurring exception workflows and integrations

The metric to prove is not "AI accuracy" alone.

The metric to prove is:

**How many traceability exceptions can one person resolve per week with your system versus without it?**

## What This Means For MVP

Your MVP should not only extract documents.

It should simulate the job of the Product Traceability Data Specialist.

MVP workflow:

1. Intake shipment records.
2. Extract KDEs.
3. Compare records.
4. Flag missing/conflicting fields.
5. Assign issue category.
6. Draft supplier follow-up.
7. Track issue status.
8. Produce clean export.
9. Produce management report.

The best MVP demo should show:

**Before:** one person manually checking supplier emails, spreadsheets, ASNs, labels, and recall requests.

**After:** one person sees an exception queue, proof-backed field extraction, supplier drafts, and clean exports.

## Product Conclusion

The jobs data says your idea is good from a metrics perspective if you frame it correctly.

Do not measure value as:

**"How many documents can AI scan?"**

Measure value as:

**"How much traceability operations labor can one person handle with our system?"**

The best proof metrics are:

- exception volume handled per person
- manual minutes saved per shipment
- KDE completeness improvement
- supplier response time improvement
- unresolved exception aging reduction
- mock recall completion time reduction
- clean export success rate
- number of supplier/customer/regulatory requests resolved

The strongest product thesis:

**Food companies are already hiring people to become traceability data operators. Your software should become the operating layer that makes each traceability operator 3-5x more productive.**
