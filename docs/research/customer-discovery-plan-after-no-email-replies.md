### Main Diagnosis

The message is thoughtful, but it asks busy operators to do mental work.

Problems:

1. **Too much terminology up front.**
   - FSMA 204
   - KDE
   - CTE
   - TLC source
   - ERP/WMS/EDI
   - ReposiTrak/iFoodDS/TraceGains/FoodLogiQ

2. **The ask is abstract.**
   - "Can I learn about your workflow?"
   - This is polite, but it does not give them a strong reason to reply.

3. **Many emails went to generic inboxes.**
   - `info@`
   - `sales@`
   - `customer.service@`
   - `consumerinfo@`
   - `hello@`
   - These rarely reach QA, FSQA, EDI, receiving, or operations leaders.

4. **The offer is not concrete enough.**
   - "Lightweight exception layer" sounds like a product concept.
   - A buyer needs an immediate artifact: audit, report, benchmark, or checklist.

5. **The email is too long for a cold first touch.**
   - Good for a warm intro.
   - Too much for a generic cold inbox.

## The Right Lesson

Do not conclude:

**"No one wants this."**

Conclude:

**"Cold email to generic inboxes is the wrong first channel for this market."**

Food distributors are relationship-driven, operational, and busy. Many are not sitting at a SaaS-buying desk waiting for FSMA 204 tools.

You need to get closer to the workflow.

## New Strategy

Your next goal is not to sell software.

Your next goal is to get **10 conversations** with people who touch:

- receiving records
- supplier ASNs
- invoices / BOLs / packing slips
- QA / FSQA
- EDI
- recalls / mock trace
- supplier compliance
- food safety plans

The best first offer:

**Free FSMA 204 Dirty Data Audit on 5 redacted shipment record sets.**

Not:

**"Can I ask research questions?"**

Say:

**"I will show you where traceability records break."**

## New Offer

### Offer Name

**FSMA 204 Dirty Data Audit**

### What You Ask For

Ask for 5 redacted shipment record sets:

- invoice
- BOL
- packing slip
- ASN / EDI sample
- label photo
- receiving record

If they cannot share documents, ask them to walk you through one fake/example shipment.

### What You Return

Return a short report:

- missing KDEs
- invoice vs ASN vs label mismatch
- supplier field gaps
- FDA-style sortable spreadsheet sample
- estimated manual cleanup time
- recommended supplier follow-up template

This makes the conversation valuable even if they do not buy.
