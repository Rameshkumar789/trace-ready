# LinkedIn Outreach Targets For Food Traceability Customer Discovery

## Purpose

Use this list to get conversations for the **FSMA 204 Dirty Data Audit** idea.

The goal is not to spam people.

The goal is to reach people who can answer:

- Where does supplier traceability data break?
- Who fixes missing lot / TLC / KDE fields?
- Who owns FSMA 204 readiness?
- What is still manual even when companies use ERP, WMS, EDI, ReposiTrak, TagOne, FoodLogiQ, iFoodDS, or similar tools?

## Best Outreach Angle

Do not lead with:

> I am building AI software for FSMA 204.

Lead with:

> I am doing a small FSMA 204 Dirty Data Audit for distributors. I review 5 redacted shipment record sets and return a missing-KDE / mismatch report.
